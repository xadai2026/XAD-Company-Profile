# Renewable Energy: Solar EPC Solutions

## Division Overview
XAD Group provides end-to-end Solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) solutions ranging from 1 KVA residential setups to 5 MW industrial systems[cite: 2]. Operations span the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Pakistan[cite: 2].

## Core Solar Services & Products
- **System Configurations:** Design and execution of On-Grid, Off-Grid, and Hybrid Solar Systems[cite: 2].
- **Equipment Supply & Installation:** Turnkey supply and mounting of Solar PV Panels, Inverters, Battery Banks, and structural mounting systems[cite: 2].
- **Project Categories:**
  - Industrial & Commercial Solar Energy Systems[cite: 2].
  - Residential Villas, Government, & Emirati Housing Solar Projects[cite: 2].
  - Solar Car Parking Canopies & Smart Energy Solutions[cite: 2].
  - Solar Telecom Towers for STC and Zain[cite: 2].
- **Lifecycle Management:** Long-term Operation & Maintenance (O&M) services for solar installations[cite: 2].
- **Compliance:** End-to-end design and execution aligned with global IEC and IEEE standards, featuring strict utility coordination (DEWA, TAQA, ADM)[cite: 2].

## Major Project Footprint
- **UAE Deployments:** Delivered projects across Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Dubai, Ruwais, Ghayathi, Sila, and Fujairah[cite: 2].
- **KSA Deployments:** NEOM Solar structure design and implementation[cite: 2].
- **Key Clients:** Etisalat (e&), Du, STC, Zain, Taqa, Huawei, SBA, Saipem, Ministry of Community Empowerment[cite: 2].
