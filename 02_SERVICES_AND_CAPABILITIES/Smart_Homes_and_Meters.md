# Smart Solutions: Smart Homes & Utility Metering

## Division Overview
XAD Group provides comprehensive smart building automation and utility metering solutions[cite: 2]. The division serves as a major deployment partner for national utility providers and telecommunications operators across the UAE[cite: 2].

## 1. Smart Homes & Building Automation (NEXhome)
XAD offers remote automation and centralized control ecosystems designed to enhance comfort, security, and efficiency[cite: 2]. 
- **NEXhome Ecosystem Features:**
  - Smart Security & Smart Door Locks[cite: 2]
  - Visual Intercom Systems[cite: 2]
  - Smart Lighting & Automated Curtains/Blinds[cite: 2]
  - HVAC Remote Control & Environmental Monitoring[cite: 2]
  - Multi-room Background Music Systems & Centralized Control[cite: 2]
  - Smart Irrigation Systems[cite: 2]
- **Key Achievements:** Successfully delivered 1,000+ homes with smart solutions[cite: 2]. XAD is a major partner of Etisalat for smart home solutions across the UAE[cite: 2].
- **Project Portfolio:** Sobha SeaHeaven Smart Homes, Cloud Residency, and custom deployments for Etisalat and Du residential customers[cite: 2].

## 2. Utility Smart Meters (Water & Electric)
- **Deployment Scope:** Supply, installation, gateway power taping, association, testing, and commissioning of water and electric smart meters[cite: 2].
- **Monitoring:** Verifying meter online status and ensuring continuous automated reading[cite: 2].
- **Key Achievements:** 
  - Rollout installation, commissioning, and online onboarding of more than **500,000+ utility meters** in less than 1 year[cite: 2].
  - Commissioning of **1,800+ Substations** with a 100% Acceptance rate and successful handover[cite: 2].
- **Key Utility Clients:** DEWA (Dubai Electricity & Water Authority), TAQA (Abu Dhabi National Energy Company), and Etisalat[cite: 2].
