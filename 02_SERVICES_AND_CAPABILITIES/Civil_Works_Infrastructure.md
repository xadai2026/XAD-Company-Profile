# Civil Works & Telecom Infrastructure Contracting

## Division Overview
XAD Group provides complete civil engineering and structural construction services required for telecommunication towers, rooftop cell sites, fiber trenching, and power distribution systems[cite: 2].

## Turnkey Tower Erection Lifecycle

### Stage 1: Site Survey, Planning & Design
- Geotechnical soil investigation, site survey, structural load analysis, CAD design engineering, and obtaining No Objection Certificates (NOCs) from civil municipalities and authorities[cite: 2].

### Stage 2: Foundation Engineering
- Deep excavation, ground compaction, steel structure rebar fixing, formwork, high-strength concrete casting, anchor bolt setting, and foundation curing[cite: 2].

### Stage 3: Tower Erection & Structural Works
- Erection of lattice towers, monopoles, stealth structures, and rooftop guyed poles[cite: 2].
- Installation of heavy-duty cable trays, ladder racks, and safety climbs[cite: 2].
- Construction of site perimeter fencing, boundary walls, shelter pads, and access roads[cite: 2].
- **Earthing & Lightning Protection:** Grounding grid installation, copper earth rod driving, bus bar grounding, and earth resistance testing[cite: 2].

### Stage 4: Electrical, Telecom Installation & Handover
- Installation of main distribution boards (SMDB), isolators, and utility power hookups[cite: 2].
- Precision core drilling, wall penetration coring, weatherproofing, and cable sealing[cite: 2].
- Telecom hardware mounting, site snag clearance, joint inspection, Provisional Acceptance Certificate (PAC), and Final Acceptance Certificate (FAC) acquisition[cite: 2].
