## Division Overview
XAD Group’s Managed Services Division provides 24/7 end-to-end network operations, service fulfillment, field maintenance, and smart fleet dispatch services to telecommunication operators and enterprise clients[cite: 2].

## Core Managed Service Pillars

### 1. Service Fulfillment & Service Assurance
- **Broadband & Voice Activation:** End-to-end provisioning, commissioning, and activation of high-speed broadband, landline telephone, IPTV, and corporate networks[cite: 2].
- **Wireless Fixed Access:** Rapid installation and commissioning of broadband and voice services using 4G/5G outdoor units and routers[cite: 2].
- **High-Profile Account Delivery:** Direct service delivery to over **850,000+ customers**, including royal palaces, Burj Khalifa, Palm Jumeirah, and diplomatic embassies[cite: 2].

### 2. First Line Maintenance (FLM) & NOC Management
- **Field Patrol & Maintenance:** 24/7/365 patrolling, routine health checks, and preventive maintenance across mobile cell sites, fiber nodes, and security infrastructure[cite: 2].
- **Ticket Resolution (Faults & Dockets):** Rapid emergency dispatch for network outages, fiber cuts, power failures, and equipment defects[cite: 2].
- **Network Operations Center (NOC):** Alarm monitoring, remote diagnostics, dispatch management, and spare parts material supply logistics[cite: 2].

### 3. Smart Fleet Solutions & Managed Mobility
- **Uber-Style Smart Fleet Dispatch:** On-demand technician vehicle dispatch powered by a dedicated smart mobile app with GPS tracking and automated job allocation[cite: 2].
- **Dispatch SLAs:**

  - **2-Hour Vehicle Replacement SLA:** Guaranteed replacement car placement within 2 hours with a 95% KPI target[cite: 2].
- **Fleet Scope:** Full management of sedans, 4x4 SUVs, pickups, and custom fiber splicing vans, covering fuel, toll (Salik), regular servicing, cleaning, and replacement[cite: 2].
