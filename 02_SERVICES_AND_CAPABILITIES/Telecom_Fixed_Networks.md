# Telecom Services: Fixed Networks & Fiber Infrastructure

## Division Overview
XAD Group is a market pioneer in fixed broadband infrastructure, managing GPON technology migrations in the region since 2010[cite: 2]. The division executes turnkey Outside Plant (OSP) civil works, Inside Plant (ISP) structured cabling, optical distribution networks, and large-scale fiber network maintenance[cite: 2].

## Service Offerings & Scope

### 1. Outside Plant (OSP) & Civil Engineering
- **Engineering & Permits:** Route survey, optical network planning, CAD design, GIS mapping, and No Objection Certificate (NOC) processing with municipal authorities[cite: 2].
- **Civil Trenching & Trenchless Technology:** Open-cut excavation, Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD), micro-trenching, duct laying, and sub-duct insertion[cite: 2].
- **Optical Infrastructure:** Fiber Distribution Hub (FDH) placement, manhole/handhole chamber construction, joint closure splicing, and infrastructure uplifting[cite: 2].

### 2. Inside Plant (ISP) & FTTX Deployment
- **FTTX Architectures:** Full execution of FTTH (Home), FTTM (Mobile Tower), FTTC (Curb), FTTP (Premises), and FTTX turnkey projects[cite: 2].
- **Active & Passive Hardware:** Optical Line Terminal (OLT) installation, optical splitter mounting, patch panel termination, and cabinet commissioning[cite: 2].
- **Building Cable Deployment:** Vertical riser cabling, floor distribution box installation, optical fiber drop cable pulling, and ONT (Optical Network Terminal) mounting[cite: 2].

### 3. Migration & Enterprise Services
- **Legacy to GPON Migration:** Multi-stage migration management from legacy copper/coaxial networks to high-speed GPON optical networks[cite: 2].
- **Structured Cabling:** Cat6/Cat6A/Cat7 copper cabling, fiber backbone cabling, server rack cable management, and high-density patching[cite: 2].
- **Data Center Telecom Works:** Fiber entry point build-out, optical main distribution frame (OMDF) installation, and interconnect cabling[cite: 2].

## Major Track Record & Key Achievements
- **Legacy Migration Pioneer:** Over 15 years of continuous GPON migration execution across the Middle East[cite: 2].
- **Large-Scale Maintenance:** Successfully maintaining fiber networks spanning up to **420 KM** in length[cite: 2].
- **Iconic Deployments:** Executed high-density FTTX infrastructure at world-renowned landmarks including **Burj Khalifa**, **Dubai Mall**, and major UAE National Banks[cite: 2].
- **Restricted Zone Execution:** Fully certified to execute fiber deployments inside military bases, international airports, Barakah Nuclear Power Plant, royal palaces, and government complexes[cite: 2].
