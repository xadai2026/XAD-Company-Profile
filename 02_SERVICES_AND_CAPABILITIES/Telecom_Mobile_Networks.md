# Telecom Services: Mobile Network Infrastructure

## Division Overview
XAD Group provides full lifecycle mobile network engineering, turnkey equipment deployment, radio network optimization, microwave transmission, civil infrastructure, and core network migrations across multi-vendor environments[cite: 2].

## Core Capabilities & Services

### 1. In-Building Solutions (IBS), DAS & Telecom Integration (TI)
- **IBS/DAS Installation:** Deployment of passive and active Distributed Antenna Systems (DAS) and IBS equipment for indoor coverage enhancement[cite: 2].
- **Survey & Design:** Site acquisition (SAQ), site validation, RF site survey, initial planning, and coverage modeling[cite: 2].
- **Multi-Vendor Deployment:** Implementation of 2G, 3G, LTE/4G, 5G, and WiMAX solutions in complex multi-vendor environments[cite: 2].
- **RF Testing & Validation:** Site Passive Intermodulation (PIM) testing, DAS antenna sweep testing using Site Master and PIM Analyzers[cite: 2].
- **Acceptance & Maintenance:** Preventive operation and maintenance, joint Provisional Acceptance Testing (PAT) and Final Acceptance Testing (FAT) with client engineers[cite: 2].

### 2. Radio Network & Microwave (MW) Engineering
- **RF Design & Optimization:** Radio network design, drive testing, data collection, post-processing, parameter tuning, and performance optimization[cite: 2].
- **RAN Operations:** BSS/RAN network SWAP projects, network expansions, capacity upgrades, and alarm monitoring/statistics analysis[cite: 2].
- **Microwave Transmission:** Line of Sight (LOS) surveys, installation, alignment, and capacity upgrades for microwave dish sizes:
  - 0.3m, 0.6m, 1.2m, 1.8m, 2.4m, and 3.0m antennas[cite: 2].
- **Maintenance:** 24/7 corrective and preventive maintenance for RAN and microwave link assets[cite: 2].

### 3. Telecom Civil Works & Tower Installation
- **Turnkey Tower Deployments:** Greenfield survey, foundation design, structural civil works, tower erection, and guyed/monopole/lattice structures[cite: 2].
- **Rooftop & Structural Civil Works:** Roof pole installation, cable tray installation, and bus bar mounting[cite: 2].
- **Power Infrastructure:** SMDB (Sub-Main Distribution Boards), isolator installation, earthing systems, and power cabling[cite: 2].
- **Civil Site Preparation:** Diamond core drilling, concrete coring, trenching, and cable entry waterproofing[cite: 2].
- **Permitting:** Complete civil documentation and authority approval management[cite: 2].

### 4. Core Networks & Datacenter Telecom Integration
- **Equipment Racking & Staging:** Central office survey, equipment rack installation, and hardware mounting[cite: 2].
- **Optical Transport:** DWDM (Dense Wavelength Division Multiplexing) equipment setup, fiber patch panels, and optical multiplexing[cite: 2].
- **IP & Routing:** Core router and switch installation, configuration, and traffic migration[cite: 2].
- **Site Security & Monitoring:** Environmental Monitoring (EM), cabling, CCTV installation inside core network shelters, PAC acceptance, and site handover[cite: 2].
