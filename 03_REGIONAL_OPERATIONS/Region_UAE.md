# Regional Operations: United Arab Emirates (UAE)

## Overview
The United Arab Emirates serves as the global headquarters and primary operational base for Xad Technologies Group. Operations span all seven Emirates through specialized corporate entities handling telecommunications, managed services, civil contracting, and automotive logistics[cite: 2].

## Branch Offices & Warehouse Depots
- **Dubai Head Office:** Al Quoz Industrial Area (Central warehousing, material supply, and administrative hub)[cite: 2].
- **Abu Dhabi Office:** ADCP Tower, Office #1401[cite: 2]. Direct oversight for Western Region utility and telecommunication projects.
- **Sharjah Hub:** Mohammed Al Mulla Tower (Al Ittihad St, Al Nahda) and Sharjah Publishing City (SPC) Free Zone.
- **Regional Depots:** Active operational yards and storage warehouses established across Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, and Al Ain[cite: 2].

## Core Regional Scope & Key Projects
- **Telecom Infrastructure:** Long-standing framework agreements with **Etisalat (e&)** and **du** for mobile network rollout, FTTH/GPON fixed network deployment, and outside plant (OSP) civil works[cite: 2].
- **Smart Metering & Grid Deployment:** Massive rollout of over 500,000+ water and electric smart meters and 1,800+ substation commissionings for **DEWA** and **TAQA**[cite: 2].
- **Smart Communities & Real Estate:** Delivery of 1,000+ smart homes (NEXhome) across premium developments like Sobha SeaHeaven and Cloud Residency[cite: 2].
- **Landmark Deployments:** Specialized installation teams deployed inside high-security and iconic locations including Burj Khalifa, Burj Al Arab, Dubai Mall, Expo 2020, Barakah Nuclear Plant, and Etihad Rail[cite: 2].
