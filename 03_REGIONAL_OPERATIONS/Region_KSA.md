# Regional Operations: Kingdom of Saudi Arabia (KSA)

## Overview
XAD Group maintains a massive operational footprint across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia through its primary entities (**Xad Riyadh** and **Speed Technologies**), delivering mobile networks, fixed fiber infrastructure, and large-scale civil works[cite: 2].

## Geographic Coverage & Branch Network
XAD KSA operates across eight major regional hubs[cite: 2]:
- **Riyadh** (Central Region HQ)[cite: 2]
- **Dammam** (Eastern Province)[cite: 2]
- **Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Taif** (Western Region & Holy Sites)[cite: 2]
- **Tabuk** (Northern Region & NEOM development sector)[cite: 2]
- **Jazan** (Southern Region)[cite: 2]

## Major Scope of Work & Services
- **Mobile & Core Network:** RAN installation, 5G IBS deployment, microwave links, data center server racking, MOP designing, and UPS installations[cite: 2].
- **Civil & Tower Erection:** Turnkey civil solutions, access road construction, tower strengthening, and erection of massive structures (such as the **NEOM 90-meter tower for STC**)[cite: 2].
- **Fixed Networks & OSP/ISP:** Trenching, ducting, backfilling, asphalt restoration, roop shooting, cable pulling, U-guard installation, DB setup, and fusion splicing for **STC, Mobily, Dawiyat, and Abana**[cite: 2].
- **Solar Energy Infrastructure:** Design, supply, and implementation of solar power systems for telecom sites and remote infrastructure[cite: 2].

## Prestigious Flagship Projects in KSA
- **Hajj Network SWAP Project:** Comprehensive mobile network optimization and infrastructure rollout across the sacred holy sites of **Mina, Muzdalifah, Arafat, and Jamarat**[cite: 2].
- **NEOM Infrastructure:** Large-scale telecom tower erection, solar system integration, and end-to-end LAN/router cabling for NEOM master developments[cite: 2].
- **Masjid Nabawi Project:** Specialized indoor/outdoor telecom coverage implementation in Madinah[cite: 2].
