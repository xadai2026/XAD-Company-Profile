# Regional Operations: United Kingdom (UK)

## Overview
XAD Group operates in the United Kingdom through **Xad Technologies London**, **Field Communication Ltd**, and **Xad Fiber UK**, providing specialized fiber network deployment, civil engineering, and environmental building services[cite: 2].

## Branch Offices & Depots
- **UK Head Office:** Kemp House, 160 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX[cite: 2].
- **Operational Depots:** Office 227 & 231, 268 Bath Road, Slough, SL1 4DX.

## Geographic Service Zones
XAD UK deploys engineering teams across England and Scotland[cite: 2]:
- **Central London** (District Heating & Smart Meter Remedial Works)[cite: 2]
- **Edinburgh & Farnborough** (FTTH Urban Deployments)[cite: 2]
- **Aberdeen, Dundee, & Lincoln** (Broadband Expansion)[cite: 2]
- **Eday Island** (Remote Island Telecom Infrastructure)[cite: 2]

## Core UK Service Offerings & Projects
- **Fixed Network (FTTH Deployment):** Working alongside Tier-1 infrastructure partners like **Circet (Ireland & UK)** and **Openreach** on the R100 and regional fiber upgrade projects[cite: 2]. Laying armored cables and executing COF 640 installations in verges[cite: 2].
- **Central London District Heating Systems (DHS):** 
  - Gas distribution for boilers (cable trays, cabling, heat meters, flow meters, distribution boxes, and labeling)[cite: 2].
  - Heat Interface Unit (HIU) installations inside residential complexes[cite: 2].
  - Essential plumbing and electrical remedial works[cite: 2].
- **Electric Meter Gateway Remedial Works:** Power-up sequences, external support antenna mounting, and gateway re-commissioning[cite: 2].
