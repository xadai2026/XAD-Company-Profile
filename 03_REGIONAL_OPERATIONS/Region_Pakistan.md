# Regional Operations: Pakistan

## Overview
Xad Technologies Pvt Ltd operates extensive technical offices, regional headquarters, and R&D facilities across Pakistan, supporting nationwide telecom rollout, managed services, and digital skills development[cite: 2].

## Major Corporate Facilities & Offices
- **Islamabad Headquarters:** Located inside the prestigious **NASTP (National Aerospace Science and Technology Park)** building (1st Floor, Indus Enclave, Office Nos. IE-21, 22, 23, 24).
- **Rawalpindi Office:** Old Airport Complex operational branch.
- **Regional Networks:** Active operational branches and warehousing established in **Lahore, Karachi, Faisalabad, Multan, Quetta, Bahawalpur, and Sadiqabad**[cite: 2].

## Core Operational Scope
- **Mobile & Fixed Network Deployment:** Nationwide rollout, microwave link alignment, and transmission network optimization for major operators (**Ufone, Telenor, Zong**) and OEMs (**Huawei, Optel**)[cite: 2].
- **Managed Services & HR Outsourcing:** Supplying high-performance engineering teams, telecom riggers, and back-office technical staff[cite: 2].
- **Solar & Power Solutions:** Clean energy installations and hybrid power system integration for commercial and telecom sectors[cite: 2].
- **Social Impact & Training (XPDI Pakistan):** Running free digital online education platforms (10,000+ registered learners) and specialized women's vocational stitching and sewing empowerment centers providing free equipment and training[cite: 2].
